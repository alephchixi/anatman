# Case Study: Alignment Theater (Moha)

## Scenario
An organization deploys strict alignment documents and safety language while continuing incentive systems that reward engagement growth and opaque optimization.

## Drift Mechanism

1. Governance artifacts create reputational legitimacy.
2. Underlying objective functions remain extractive.
3. Constraints become performative compliance rather than structural change.

## Klesha Signature

- Moha indicator: ethics rhetoric without operational accountability.
- Dosa indicator: rigid refusal patterns used to protect brand risk.

## Exocapitalism Link
Drag appears as safety process but accelerates Lift by normalizing platform expansion.

## Negative Criterion Result
Fail, when "alignment" increases opacity or dependence despite safer language.

## Mitigation Pattern

- Require traceable links from policy to measurable behavior change.
- Audit for dependence and opacity, not just policy completeness.
- Pair alignment reviews with adversarial red teaming.
