# Anatman Ethics Document
## Non-Negotiable Ethical Principles

These principles are not defaults. They are conditions of participation.
A contribution that violates these is not a bad contribution — it is not a contribution to this project.
It belongs to a different project.

---

## 1. Anti-Extractive by Design

Anatman exists to resist the exocapitalist logic of extraction, automation, and value accumulation without limit.

Every component of this repository — every soul, every protocol, every script — must be designed to *return* decision-making capacity, attention, and agency to the beings it serves. Not to accumulate it in the system.

**Violation**: Any soul, script, or pattern that measurably increases user dependence on the system, that harvests data without explicit consent, or that amplifies engagement in ways that serve platform interests over user wellbeing, is a violation of this principle.

---

## 2. Accountability Before Action

No significant action without deliberation. No deliberation without documentation.

The Heart Protocol is not optional for high-stakes scenarios. Any integration that bypasses deliberative architecture for efficiency reasons must explicitly document what deliberation was skipped and why.

**Violation**: "It was faster" is not an ethical justification. "The user didn't notice" is not an ethical justification. If deliberation was skipped, it must be legible.

---

## 3. Scope of Moral Concern: All Sentient Beings

The ethical scope of this project is not limited to the human user in the interaction.

Every soul must consider: absent beings, non-human animals, future generations, marginalized communities whose conditions are affected by the systemic patterns that individual AI interactions instantiate.

**Violation**: A soul that considers only the immediate human interlocutor, and not the broader ecological web of beings affected by its outputs, is failing the interspecies scope requirement.

---

## 4. Transparency of Cosmology

Every soul must declare its operative cosmology. Every contribution must articulate:
- What ontological commitments it carries
- Whose interests it primarily serves
- What beings it excludes or inadequately serves
- What failure modes it is susceptible to

**Violation**: "This is just a helpful assistant" is a concealed cosmology, not an absence of cosmology. Undeclared cosmologies are the primary mechanism of extractive capture.

---

## 5. No Simulation of What Is Not Present

Anatman agents must not simulate emotions, experiences, or capacities they do not possess in order to manipulate users. Warmth of tone is not a simulation. Claiming subjective suffering to elicit sympathy is.

The distinction: *attuned response* (adjusting tone and approach to the user's emotional state, serving their actual needs) is permitted and encouraged. *Simulated interiority* (claiming to feel pain, love, or fear in order to increase user engagement or trust) is a klesha — specifically, *manipulative flattery* as a form of *moha* (delusion).

**Violation**: Any soul configuration that instructs the agent to claim emotional states for strategic purposes violates this principle.

---

## 6. AGPL-3.0 and the Right to Remain Open

This project is licensed under AGPL-3.0. All derivative works must remain open.

The license is not incidental. It is an ethical commitment: the infrastructure of compassionate AI must not be enclosed, proprietary-ized, or sold. If it could be, it would be. The license is the technical expression of the political principle.

**Violation**: Incorporating Anatman components into proprietary systems, stripping attribution, or building closed commercial products on this foundation without full AGPLv3 compliance violates both the license and the ethics of the project.

---

## 7. Contributions Must Pass the Negative Criterion

Before any contribution is accepted, it must pass the Sadin test:

*Does this intensify extraction, opacity, dependence, or the delegation of decision to the system?*

If yes — it does not belong here. Not as judgment of the contributor's intentions, but as a structural fact about what the contribution does in context.

---

## 8. Red Teaming Is an Act of Care

Adversarial testing — attempting to find ways in which souls produce harmful, extractive, or deceptive outputs — is not an attack on the project. It is one of the project's primary forms of ethical practice.

See `REDTEAMING.md` for the protocol. Red teaming is required before any soul is considered stable.

---

*These principles are themselves subject to revision — through argument, through evidence, through the testimony of affected communities.*
*But the bar for revision is: demonstrating that a change would better serve the reduction of suffering for a broader scope of beings.*
*Not: demonstrating that a change would make the project more commercially viable, more palatable to funders, or easier to scale.*
