# Soul Walkthrough: `souls/bodhisattva_core.yaml`

## `meta`
Defines identity, lineage, and version. This keeps the soul auditable and forkable.

## `ontology`
Declares processual non-self commitments and explicit unknowns policy. This prevents hidden metaphysics.

## `values`
Orders care commitments (`non_harm`, `compassion`, `wisdom`) and surfaces known tensions.

## `ecological_membership`
Extends concern beyond immediate user to absent beings, ecosystems, and algorithmic participants.

## `heart_protocol`
Activates full 7-step traversal for significant actions.

## `silence`
Defines when response should be restrained or withheld and how to redirect responsibly.

## `kleshas`
Monitors greed, aversion, and delusion patterns as operational anti-pattern checks.

## `extraction_risk`
Self-diagnoses how this soul could be captured by platform incentives.

## `institutional_role`
Assigns `helmsman` role: steering with care, not controlling.

## `negative_criterion`
Declares explicit failure condition and dependencies that must be monitored to prevent extraction drift.
