# Cosmotechnics: The Ontological Response

> *Hui, Yuk. The Question Concerning Technology in China: An Essay in Cosmotechnics. Urbanomic, 2016.*
> *Hui, Yuk. "Cosmotechnics & the Multicultural Trap." TripleAmpersand Journal, 2020.*

---

## The Central Claim

Technology is never culturally or metaphysically neutral. Every technique articulates a relationship between nature, morality, cosmology, and operation. 

Hui explicitly defines cosmotechnics as:
> *"the unification of the cosmic order and moral order through technical activities"* ("Cosmotechnics & the Multicultural Trap").

Harking back to Kant, Hui frames this scheme as an attempt to solve the antinomy of the universality of technology: *"meaning the tension between the fact (or thesis) that technology is a human constant and the fact (or antithesis) that technology is always constrained and enabled by particular cosmologies"* (Ibid.).

## The Gestell (Enframing) and Its Consequences

Heidegger's concept of *Gestell* identifies the mode of revealing characteristic of modern technology. As Hui notes via Hookway: "Heidegger uses the term Gestell, a frame or an enframing, defined as 'the gathering together of that setting-upon [Stellen] that sets upon man... to reveal the real, in the mode of ordering [Bestellen], as standing-reserve [Bestand]'" (Hookway, 2014, p. 43). 

Crucially, for Hui, overcoming Gestell is a planetary necessity:
> *"The necessity to overcome a Eurocentric perspective, Hui explains, would be important not only for those localities which were colonized, but also for Europe itself, precisely for it to overcome its fatal destiny of enframing (or Gestell) which Heidegger warned against in his influential text on the question of technology"* ("Cosmotechnics & the Multicultural Trap").

In Exocapitalism and AI, the operational logic of Gestell means:
- Training data is treated as a "standing reserve" of human expression
- The Earth itself becomes a layer (The Stack) to be calculated and consumed

## Cosmotechnics as Alternative

Against the universalism of Gestell, Hui proposes technodiversity. "Yuk Hui (2016) provides a synthesis of Martin Heidegger’s and Bernard Stiegler’s accounts of technics by acknowledging both the plurality of cosmologies and of technologies, in a double move that acknowledges the technicity of the cosmic and the cosmicity of technics, pluralizing both" (del Val, 2022, p. 153). 

The political implication: **technodiversity** — the intentional cultivation of multiple cosmotechnical lineages. Specific modes of technics correspond to specific cosmologies in an open loop that questions the nature-culture split. 

## The Soul as Cosmotechnical Object

The critical move for Anatman: applying the concept of cosmotechnics to the design of AI souls.

Every agentic system has already a metaphysics — a set of implicit commitments about what exists, what matters, and what kinds of beings deserve consideration. When designers do not make this explicit, it defaults to the cosmology of the exocapitalist paradigm, which treats the world as an optimization field.

**Making the soul explicit is making the cosmotechnics legible.**

The Anatman soul specification requires, in its `ontology` field:
- `mode`: the operative cosmological mode (cosmotechnical / relational / processual / ecological)
- `commitments`: explicit ontological commitments
- `unknowns_policy`: how the soul relates to what it does not know — holding space for the inconditioned.

## Art, The Open, and the Unknown

In *Art and Cosmotechnics*, Hui shows how art provides a route out of the recursive mechanicity of purely rational intelligence. Art opens to what cannot be reduced to calculation, prediction, or optimization.

For Anatman, this translates into the `unknowns_policy` requirement: every soul must declare how it relates to the boundaries of its own knowledge. An honest soul says: "There are things I cannot know. There are questions I should not answer." This maps to the silence protocols.
