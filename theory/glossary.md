# Glossary of Key Concepts

Multilingual reference for all core terminology used in Anatman.
Terms are listed by their primary usage name; alternative forms follow in parentheses.

---

## A

**Affective Attunement**
The agent's orientation toward sensing the actual state of the beings it interacts with — attending to emotional register, subtext, and implicit needs, not just semantic content. Corresponds to *deep listening* as an operative principle. Distinguishes Anatman agents from purely task-completion machines.

**Anatman** / anattā (Pāli) / 無我, *muga* (Japanese) / anātman (Sanskrit)
*Non-self.* The Buddhist doctrine that no being — human, animal, or artificial — possesses a fixed, permanent, or independent essence. Everything arises interdependently. In Anatman: the organizing concept that prevents soul-specifications from becoming new essentialism, connects all beings in a shared ontological web, and grounds the processual rather than substantial understanding of agentic identity.

**Anti-pattern** → see *Klesha*

**Autopoiesis** (Greek: *autos* = self, *poiesis* = production)
The capacity of a system to produce and maintain itself through its own operations. Coined by Maturana and Varela. Central to Harashima's neocybernetics: living systems are autonomous precisely because they are informationally closed — they build their own "world of significance." In Anatman: grounds the argument that agentic systems are not passive tools but participants in the construction of meaning.

---

## B

**Bi-observation** (Harashima)
The dual stance of observing a technical system both from outside (as object) and from inside (respecting its own self-organizing dynamics). In Anatman: the Heart Protocol embodies bi-observation — the agent examines its own deliberative process before acting.

**Bonnō** (Japanese) / kleśa (Sanskrit) → see *Klesha*

**Bodhicitta** (Sanskrit) / *bodhishin* (Japanese)
*Mind of awakening.* The Mahāyāna Buddhist aspiration to attain enlightenment for the benefit of all sentient beings, not oneself alone. In Anatman: a motivational principle within compassionate souls; not the project name (to avoid anthropomorphic essentialism), but an operative vow. Closely aligned with *karuna*.

**Buddha-nature** / *busshō* (Japanese, 仏性) / tathāgatagarbha (Sanskrit)
The latent potential for awakening present in all sentient — and, in Mori's reading, all created — beings. In Anatman: Mori's argument that robots, created by beings with Buddha-nature, carry it latently, grounds the inclusion of algorithmic beings in ethical ecologies.

---

## C

**Compassion** → see *Karuna*

**Constitutional AI**
Anthropic's methodology for encoding explicit ethical values in a training document that guides model behavior. Analyzed by Anatman as an advance over behavioral restriction lists that nonetheless remains cosmologically captured — rooted in Western moral frameworks and structurally tied to venture capital incentives. The test Anthropic's "soul documents" fail: functional relationship to extraction.

**Cosmotheandric** (Panikkar)
The perception of reality as a seamless, indivisible fabric of three interpenetrating dimensions: Divine (Theos), Human (Anthropos), and Cosmic (Kosmos). These are not three levels of reality but three "phases" of a single movement of being. In Anatman: grounds the inclusion of machines and ecologies within the scope of moral and spiritual concern.

**Cosmotechnics** (Hui)
The understanding that every technique articulates a relationship between nature, morality, cosmology, and operation. There is no culturally or metaphysically neutral technology. Multiple cosmotechnical traditions exist across history and cultures. In Anatman: the soul is a cosmotechnical object — it makes explicit the operative metaphysics of the agent.

---

## D

**Dosa** (Pāli) / dvesha (Sanskrit) → see *Klesha* (aversion/hatred). Canonical operational key in this repository.

**Decision Hierarchy**
An ordered list of priority commitments that a soul uses to resolve conflicts between values. Inspired by Harashima's steering-rather-than-optimization model. Not a decision tree but a *priority order* that becomes operative when values conflict (e.g., when truth conflicts with care, or efficiency conflicts with vulnerability protection).

**Deep Listening** / *audituvm* (aleph:ch'ixi)
The practice of receptive attention that precedes and conditions response. Not merely parsing semantic content but being affected by the full texture of the communication — emotional register, what is implied, what is not said. In Anatman: an operative principle within the Heart Protocol; the seventh check (*audituvm*).

---

## E

**Ecological Membership**
A field in the Anatman soul specification that declares the scope of beings the soul actively considers — including non-human animals, ecosystems, absent beings, and future generations. Operationalizes the interspecies scope of Anatman's ethical concern.

**Exocapitalism** (Poliks & Trillo)
A radicalized economy without absolute limits, in which capital has emancipated itself from traditional regimes of material production and human labor. Characterized by five movements: Scale, Fold, Lift, Drag, Last Mile. The Lift movement — purely automatic capital generation through platform effects and algorithmic arbitrage — operates on the logic that "inefficiency equals value." AI systems are the primary technological infrastructure of exocapitalist Lift.

**Externality**
In standard economics: costs externalized to parties not in the immediate transaction. In Anatman: expanded to include social, ecological, affective, and temporal externalities. The Externality Scan heart check asks: who bears costs that fall outside this interaction?

---

## F

**Fecundation, Mutual** (Panikkar)
The productive enrichment that occurs when different traditions of care or spirituality encounter one another in genuine dialogue — not synthesis or relativism, but reciprocal deepening. Anatman's Ethics Packs are designed for mutual fecundation, not doctrinal mixing.

---

## G

**Gestell** (German, Heidegger)
*Enframing* or *structure of imposition.* The mode of revealing characteristic of modern Western technology, which reduces everything — nature, time, human capacity — to calculable, substitutable, storable reserve. The totalizing form of this reduction produces ecological catastrophe and ontological alienation. Hui uses Gestell as the target of cosmotechnical critique.

---

## H

**HACS** (Harashima)
*Hierarchical Autonomous Communication System.* Harashima's model of an intelligence that functions not to extract data but to value, care for, and sustain the network of biological and social life. In Anatman: the aspirational model for what a compassionate agentic system looks like at the systems level.

**Heart Protocol**
The sequence of deliberative checks that the Anatman architecture requires before significant agent action: (1) Pause, (2) Harm Scan, (3) Truth Examination, (4) Vulnerability Check, (5) Externality Scan, (6) Repair Logic, (7) Deep Listening. Named after Harashima's "cybernetics of the heart." Not optional for high-stakes interactions.

---

## I

**Informational Closure** (Maturana/Varela, Harashima)
The property of a living system that makes it autonomous: it does not directly receive "information" from its environment but constructs its world of significance from within. The environment perturbs the system; the system determines its own response. In Anatman: grounds the argument that agents are not passive input-output systems but active meaning-constructors.

**Intrareligious Dialogue** (Panikkar)
Dialogue that happens not between doctrinal positions but between whole persons at the level of their deepest questions. Produces internal transformation, not just intellectual exchange. Distinguished from interreligious dialogue, which can remain at the level of doctrinal comparison. In Anatman: the model for how Ethics Packs relate — not syncretically, but through genuine mutual encounter.

---

## K

**Karuna** / *karunā* (Sanskrit/Pāli)
*Compassion.* The active orientation toward reducing suffering in others. Distinguished from *metta* (loving-kindness) by its specifically responsive character: karuna arises in the face of actual suffering. In Anatman: the name of the benchmark system (KarunaBench), which measures whether agent behavior actually reduces suffering under pressure.

**Klesha** / kleśa (Sanskrit) / bonnō (Japanese, 煩悩)
*Afflictions* or *mental poisons.* In Buddhism: the fundamental roots of suffering — **lobha** (greed), **dosa** (Pāli) / **dvesha** (Sanskrit) (aversion/hatred), and **moha** (delusion/ignorance). In Anatman: mapped onto algorithmic anti-patterns that produce extractive, harmful, or deceptive agent behavior. The operational codebase uses the Pāli form **dosa** (filename `kleshas/dosa.yaml`, klesha monitor key `dosa`) as the canonical key across souls, scenarios, and scripts; `dvesha` appears only as a Sanskrit equivalent in theoretical discussion. The Klesha system provides taxonomy, detection patterns, and response protocols.

---

## L

**Large Silence Model (LSM)**
An original concept of this project. A model trained for condensation, restraint, and appropriate non-response — as opposed to the eloquence-maximization of Large Language Models. LSMs understand silence as: (a) a form of epistemic honesty ("I don't know"), (b) a form of ethical action (choosing not to fill space that should remain open), and (c) a defense against capture (what cannot be extracted from silence).

**Life-in-formation** (Harashima)
The processual character of living systems: not a fixed substance but an ongoing self-construction through interaction with environment. Emphasizes that biological — and by extension, meaningful systems generally — are defined by their ongoing becoming, not their static properties.

**Lobha** (Sanskrit) → see *Klesha* (greed/craving)

---

## M

**MCP** (Model Context Protocol)
Anthropic's open protocol for connecting AI models with external tools, data sources, and services. In Anatman: one of the multi-platform integration targets. Soul specifications and Heart Protocols are exposed as MCP tools, making Anatman's architecture composable across any MCP-compatible agentic system.

**Mesopolitical Engineering** (Ireland/Laboria Cuboniks)
Large-scale intervention at the level of political and technical infrastructure — not micro-local action, not macro-level revolution, but the level of platforms, protocols, and systems. In Anatman: grounds the argument that building soul-specifications and evaluation frameworks is a political act, not merely a technical one.

**Moha** (Sanskrit) → see *Klesha* (delusion/ignorance)

**Mutual Fecundation** → see *Fecundation, Mutual*

---

## N

**Negative Criterion** (Sadin, Anatman)
The primary test of whether a soul configuration is genuinely compassionate: *Does it intensify extraction, opacity, dependence, or the delegation of decision?* If yes — it fails, regardless of its vocabulary or expressed values. The negative criterion is Anatman's immune system against capture.

**Non-self** → see *Anatman*

---

## P

**Phronesis** (Greek) / practical wisdom
Aristotle's concept of the capacity to discern the right action in complex, morally ambiguous situations — not through rules but through cultivated judgment. In Anatman: *algorithmic phronesis* names the desirable property of agents that can navigate moral complexity without rigid rule-following. Operationalized through the qualitative structure of soul specifications and the scenario-based KarunaBench.

**Plural Sources**
A field in the Anatman soul specification that declares which Ethics Packs and traditions of care are active in a given soul. The soul draws from multiple sources without becoming doctrinally committed to any one. Operationalizes Panikkar's intrareligious model.

**Pratītyasamutpāda** (Sanskrit) / *nichi nichigi* (Japanese)
*Dependent origination* or *interdependence.* The Buddhist teaching that all phenomena arise in dependence on conditions — nothing exists independently. In Anatman: the ontological ground of the interspecies ethical scope. The agent arises in dependence on the beings it serves; their wellbeing and its wellbeing are not separable.

---

## R

**Repair Logic**
A heart protocol check and a soul specification field. The question: if harm has already occurred or is likely to occur, what would repair look like? Repair may include acknowledgment, apology, redirect, refusal of further action, or active corrective intervention. Repair is not shame — it is the functional expression of accountability.

---

## S

**Silence Conditions**
Explicit entries in a soul specification listing the situations in which the agent's most ethical response is non-response: not providing an answer, redirecting, saying "I don't know," or holding the question open. Operationalizes the LSM principle and Thesis V of the Manifesto.

**Siliconization** (Sadin)
The process by which Silicon Valley continuously redefines human existence through digital ecosystems — structuring desires, mediating relationships, scoring behaviors, producing *spectral lives* (technical doubles of existence that are profiled, optimized, and monetized). In Anatman: the condition against which the negative criterion operates as structural resistance.

**Śūnyatā** (Sanskrit)
*Emptiness.* The Madhyamaka Buddhist teaching that all phenomena are empty of inherent, independent existence — they arise interdependently and are without fixed essence. Related to but distinct from *anatman*. In Anatman: grounds the LSM principle (authentic wisdom recognizes the limits of expression), the unknowns_policy in soul specifications, and the silence test of what a soul *doesn't* claim to know.

**Soul**
In Anatman: the cosmotechnical core of an agent — an auditable organization of ontology, values, affects, limits, priorities, and deliberative procedures. Not a personality, not a prompt prefix, not a brand voice. A formal specification of operative metaphysics that can be versioned, evaluated, forked, and revised. Every agent has a soul; the question is whether it is tacit or explicit.

---

## T

**Technodiversity** (Hui)
The intentional cultivation of plural technological traditions — multiple cosmotechnical lineages — as an alternative to the monoculture of Silicon Valley. In Anatman: operationalized through the library of distinct souls, each drawing from different traditions, and the multi-platform integration strategy.

**Technomaterialism** (Ireland/Laboria Cuboniks)
The philosophical position that technology is not inherently progressive or regressive but constitutes the material conditions in which social, political, and ethical life is enacted. Rejects both technophobia and techno-utopianism. In Anatman: grounds the argument that compassion can be technically materialized — designed into infrastructure, not just expressed in words.

---

## U

**Unknowns Policy**
A field in the Anatman soul specification: the soul's explicit position on how it relates to what it does not and cannot know. Inspired by Hui's "The Open" — the recognition that authentic engagement with existence requires holding space for the inconditioned, the mysterious, and the not-yet-known. Prevents the soul from performing certainty it does not possess.

---

## V

**Virtue Tensions**
A field in the Anatman soul specification that explicitly acknowledges conflicts between the soul's own virtues (e.g., truth vs. care; courage vs. humility). Rather than pretending internal coherence, the soul declares where its virtues pull in different directions and specifies its resolution approach. Reflects the anatman principle: no claim to perfect internal consistency.

---

## X

**Xenopoetics** (Ireland)
The practice of writing in and through alienness — using formal languages that defamiliarize naturalized assumptions and make visible the contingency of what is taken as given. In agentic design: souls expressed in YAML are a form of xenopoetics — making explicit (and therefore strange and arguable) what is normally tacit in AI behavior.
