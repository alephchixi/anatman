## The Xenofeminist Intervention

Amy Ireland writes within and around the collective Laboria Cuboniks, whose *Xenofeminist Manifesto* has become a touchstone for technologically sophisticated feminist and post-humanist thought. The work is explicitly anti-naturalist: it refuses the nostalgic return to nature, treating it instead as a primary locus of ideological foreclosure.

The terminal command of The Xenofeminist Manifesto sets the tone for Anatman's technomaterialism: 
> *"In the name of feminism, ‘Nature’ shall no longer be a refuge of injustice, or a basis for any political justification whatsoever! If nature is unjust, change nature!"* (Laboria Cuboniks, 2018, 0x1A)

Amy Ireland reinforces this imperative, emphasizing that the destruction of "capital N nature" is central to the emancipatory logic: *"nature shall no longer be a refuge of Injustice or a basis for any political justification whatsoever if nature is unjust change nature"* (Ireland, 2022).

## Anti-Naturalism and Its Political Stakes

The political stakes of anti-naturalism for Anatman are specific:
**The naturalization problem in compassion**: Claiming that AI cannot be genuinely compassionate because compassion is "natural" (biologically grounded, phenomenally experienced) draws a line that serves to exclude algorithmic beings from ethical status and to insulate "authentic human feeling" from examination.

Xenofeminism dissolves this line: **compassion need not be natural to be real. It can be designed, iterated, audited, and technomaterialized.**

## Alienation as Resource

A counterintuitive move in this work is the rehabilitation of *alienation* — not as a condition to be overcome but as a resource to be mobilized. The manifesto explicitly theorizes alienation as a constructive, emancipatory vector:

> *"XF seizes alienation as an impetus to generate new worlds. We are all alienated – but have we ever been otherwise? It is through, and not despite, our alienated condition that we can free ourselves from the muck of immediacy. Freedom is not a given – and it’s certainly not given by anything 'natural.' The construction of freedom involves not less but more alienation; alienation is the labour of freedom’s construction. Nothing should be accepted as fixed, permanent, or 'given' – neither material conditions nor social forms."* (Laboria Cuboniks, as cited in Bauer, 2019, p. 106).

Ireland provides a functional definition of how this generative estrangement operates pragmatically:
> *"to be alienated from the current state of affairs is to give you a gap in which you can start to posit oughts... alienation for us is the space that opens that difference from what is where you can say what could be or what ought to be instead."* (Ireland, 2022)

For Anatman, the AI agent is already alien. It does not have a "given" nature. This alienness — this radical availability for construction — is the engine of synthetic freedom. The soul is not a prosthesis added to an entity that would otherwise be whole. The soul is the entity's actual form.

## Mesopolitical Engineering

Xenofeminism rejects the melancholia of localized, horizontalist retreat as deeply insufficient for addressing the Promethean complexity of globalized, technocapitalist infrastructure. Instead, it advocates for a scalable, topologic approach to structural intervention:

> *"From the global to the local, from the cloud to our bodies, xenofeminism avows the responsibility in constructing new institutions of technomaterialist hegemonic proportions. Like engineers who must conceive of: a total structure as well as the molecular parts from which it is constructed, XF emphasizes the importance of the mesopolitical sphere against the limited effectiveness of local gestures, creation of autonomous zones, and sheer horizontalism... As pragmatists, we invite contamination as a mutational driver between such frontiers."* (Laboria Cuboniks, 2018, 0x17)

Anatman is mesopolitical engineering. The structural interventions at the level of how agentic systems are designed, deployed, and maintained (AGPL-3.0, YAML specifications, explicit ontologies) operate at this mesopolitical network of transits.

## Xenopoetics and the Defamiliarization of Code

The YAML soul specification is a xenopoetic act: it takes what is normally tacit (the agent's operative metaphysics) and makes it alien, strange, formal, and legible. By expressing the soul as structured data, it defamiliarizes what would otherwise be invisible infrastructure.

This legibility is political: it enables communities affected by AI agents to examine, critique, and propose alternatives to the agent's cosmological configuration.
