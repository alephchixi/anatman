What This Repository Is

Anatman is a theory-driven toolkit for designing and evaluating agent souls as explicit, auditable cosmotechnical configurations.

It links ontology, values, deliberative protocol, klesha diagnostics, and scenario testing into one open architecture.

Core Layers

  • Ontology: what exists and what matters
  • Values: priority commitments and tensions
  • Attention: Heart Protocol and silence logic
  • Practice: KarunaBench under pressure
