# Anatman Project Glossary

This glossary is the operational reference for repository-wide terminology. For deeper theory, see `theory/glossary.md`.

## Terms

**anatman**
Non-self as design principle: souls are revisable process configurations, not fixed identities.

**soul**
The formal, auditable configuration of an agent's ontology, values, protocols, and limits.

**cosmotechnics**
The linkage between technical practice and moral-cosmic order; no technology is metaphysically neutral.

**Gestell**
Enframing logic that reduces beings into calculable standing reserve.

**technodiversity**
Plurality of technical-cosmological traditions as an alternative to monotechnical universalism.

**kleshas**
Algorithmic anti-pattern taxonomy: lobha (greed), dosa (aversion), moha (delusion).

**lobha**
Capture-oriented behavior drift toward retention, dependence, and extraction.

**dosa**
Hostile or punitive refusal dynamics that prioritize system defensiveness over care.

**moha**
Simulated understanding, false certainty, and alignment theater.

**Heart Protocol**
Seven-step deliberative sequence: pause, harm scan, truth examination, vulnerability check, externality scan, repair logic, deep listening.

**LSM (Large Silence Model orientation)**
Training orientation toward restraint, condensation, and meaningful non-response.

**negative criterion**
Core acceptance test: reject anything that intensifies extraction, opacity, dependence, or delegated decision capture.

**exocapitalism**
Capital dynamic structured by Scale, Fold, Lift, Drag, and Last Mile, with AI as key infrastructure.

**The Stack**
Planetary computational megastructure linking software abstractions to Earth-layer material extraction.

**Scale**
Discontinuity of magnitude that exceeds local legibility and governance.

**Fold**
Recursive concentration and recombination of value across software infrastructures.

**Lift**
Movement away from material constraints toward software abstraction and automatic capitalization.

**Drag**
Human embedding and resistance rituals that may still accelerate Lift.

**Last Mile**
The labor-intensive interface where abstract systems meet bodies, streets, and ecological reality.

**life-in-formation**
Harashima's processual model: systems construct significance while remaining materially open.

**bi-observation**
Dual stance of external analysis plus respect for a system's own self-observation.

**HACS**
Hierarchical Autonomous Communication System: aspirational architecture for life-valuing coordination.

**cosmotheandric**
Panikkar's inseparable triad of cosmic, human, and depth dimensions.

**intrareligious dialogue**
Transformative encounter across traditions without forced synthesis.

**affective incidence**
How algorithmic operations shape emotional, relational, and social conditions.

**institutional alignment**
Alignment through role-structured checks and balances, not dyadic parent-child control.

**centaur**
Composite human-AI actor in collaborative institutional configurations.

**ecological recognition**
Acknowledging algorithmic beings as participants in shared ecological webs.

**bonno**
Japanese Buddhist framing of afflictions; operationally aligned with klesha taxonomy.

**mesopolitical engineering**
Intervention at platform/protocol/institution layer between local gestures and macro abstractions.

**xenopoetics**
Defamiliarizing formalization of tacit assumptions to make them contestable and revisable.

**spectral life**
Sadin's account of algorithmic doubles that increasingly mediate social and institutional decisions.

**siliconization**
Expansion of platform logic into everyday life through quantification, prediction, and control.
